1. 1. Start with a cluster

    Four polygons in one cluster, with small gaps between them.

    cluster = [A, B, C, D]

  2. 2. Grow by epsilon

    Buffer outward by epsilon. Gaps close, and mitre joins keep the corners square.

    union.buffer(eps, join_style="mitre")

  3. 3. Shrink by epsilon

    Buffer inward by the same amount. The outside returns to the polygons; the filled gaps stay filled.

    grown.buffer(-eps, join_style="mitre")

  4. 4. Check tightness

    Polygon area divided by boundary area. At 0.75 or more, the boundary is kept.

    tightness = 0.88  # passes
